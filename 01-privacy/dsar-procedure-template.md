# Data Subject Request (DSAR) Procedure

**Organisation:** VitaClin (fictitious) | **Version:** `<v1.0>` | **Owner:** Data Protection Officer (Encarregado)

Legal reference: LGPD arts. 18 and 19; GDPR arts. 12 to 22.

## 1. Rights covered

| Right | LGPD | GDPR |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmation and access | Art. 18, I and II | Art. 15 |
| Rectification | Art. 18, III | Art. 16 |
| Anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data | Art. 18, IV | Art. 17 / 18 |
| Portability | Art. 18, V | Art. 20 |
| Deletion of data processed with consent | Art. 18, VI | Art. 17 |
| Information about sharing | Art. 18, VII | Art. 15(1)(c) |
| Information on refusing consent and its consequences | Art. 18, VIII | Art. 7 |
| Withdrawal of consent | Art. 18, IX | Art. 7(3) |
| Review of automated decisions | Art. 20 | Art. 22 |
| Objection | Art. 18, § 2 | Art. 21 |

## 3. Workflow

| Step | Action | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Register the request (ticket number, date, channel) | Privacy team | Same day |
| 2 | Verify identity (proportionate: never ask for more data than needed) | Privacy team | `<x days>` |
| 3 | Locate the data (use the ROPA to know which systems hold it) | System owners | `<x days>` |
| 4 | Assess exceptions (legal retention, e.g. health records retention rules) | DPO / legal | `<x days>` |
| 5 | Execute and answer the data subject | Privacy team | LGPD: check art. 19 and ANPD rules; GDPR: 1 month (art. 12(3)) |
| 6 | Notify processors and recipients when data is corrected or deleted | Privacy team | |
| 7 | Close and keep evidence (without keeping more data than needed) | Privacy team | |

## 4. Response templates

- Confirmation of receipt
- Request for identity verification
- Answer (access / rectification / deletion done)
- Partial or full refusal, with the legal reason

## 5. Metrics

Number of requests per type, average response time, requests answered late.
